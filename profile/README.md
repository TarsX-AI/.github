# TarsX

**Evidence. Clear decisions. Focused implementation.**

TarsX helps organizations understand business questions through research and data, turn findings into useful decisions, and implement improvements where the evidence supports them. We use AI when it helps solve the problem and provide continuing support where it adds value.

## Current focus: GEO + AEO for hospitals

Our current delivery focus is helping hospitals be found and described accurately across AI assistants, search, maps and public listings.

- **Assess:** check discoverability, identity, published facts and cited sources.
- **Prioritize:** produce an evidence-backed report and a focused correction plan.
- **Coordinate changes:** work with the hospital and its website or platform team on approved corrections.
- **Verify:** check published changes and repeat comparable measurements, recording coverage and limitations.

GEO means generative engine optimization; AEO means answer engine optimization. These methods support discoverability and accurate answers. They do not guarantee rankings, recommendations or enquiries.

## Supporting work

- **Business intelligence:** research and authorized business or operational audits using aggregate, non-identifying data.
- **Focused implementation:** select and implement useful tools or workflows for diagnosed needs.
- **Continuing support:** defined follow-up and measurement where justified by the engagement.
- **Eligible AI advertising:** feasibility and scoped support where the market, platform and category permit it.

Our broader direction is cross-sector. Hospitals are the current focus; additional sectors are future work.
